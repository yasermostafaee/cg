# Seat the reserve's independently published Playlist output

## Why

The reserve published a valid clean Playlist in its own D10, but CG only logged that source.
Its graphic and clips appeared while its Playlist plate stayed empty.

## What Changes

- Resolve a reserve Playlist from the reserve's independently authenticated D10 and mapped channels.
- Seat its own qualified route with a pinned, unjournaled LOADBG/PLAY pair.
- Admit current, acknowledged route seats to existing paired geometry and preset motion proofs.
- Preserve the prohibition on copying arbitrary primary holder routes to the reserve.

## Impact

Bridge route seating, reserve destination guards, pinned adapter replicas, and route regressions.
No UI intent change and no claim of hardware frame synchronization.
