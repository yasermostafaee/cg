## Why

Designer can place a sound whose duration exceeds the composition's active range. Timeline Play ends with the playhead, so the author can miss the end of the sound. The Timeline row also lacks a source duration.

## What Changes

- Offer **Extend the composition** or **Cancel** when placing an oversized sound.
- Show the sound's measured duration beside its name on the Timeline.

## Capabilities

- `designer-audio-element`

## Impact

Designer sound placement, the duration dialog and Timeline rows.
