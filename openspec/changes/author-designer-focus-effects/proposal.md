## Why

Designer authors need to choose how Focus operations look when an operator uses their layout in CG Control.

## What Changes

- Let authors designate matching single-plate presets as the Focus destinations of a multi-plate preset.
- Author Focus and Return motion and the effect used between Focus destinations in the composition Inspector.
- Carry these settings into the exported VCG and use them in the existing independent PVW and PGM workflows.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `designer-focus-effects`: author named destinations and effects for Focus on a Designer preset.

## Impact

Designer Inspector and project state, shared schemas, VCG export, CG Control and CG Bridge.
